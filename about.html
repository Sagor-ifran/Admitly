About us

Admitly is a private university admission preparation platform designed to help students prepare with confidence through structured lessons, targeted practice, realistic mock exams, and actionable performance insights. From topic-wise lessons to full-length mock exams, Admitly helps students identify their weaknesses, track their progress, and prepare more strategically.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make admission preparation more accessible, organized, and effective so students can focus on learning, improving, and achieving their academic goals.

What We Offer

Admitly provides topic-wise practice, question banks, mock tests, learning resources, performance analysis, and AI-powered essay feedback to support students throughout their preparation journey.

Our Approach

We believe that consistent practice and understanding mistakes are key to improvement. That's why Admitly helps students identify their strengths, work on their weaknesses, and track their progress over time.

Who We Serve

Admitly is built for students preparing for university admission tests, starting with a focus on NSU admission preparation. We aim to expand our learning resources to support more university students such as BRAC, EWU, IUB in the future.

Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a helpful, student-friendly learning experience and continuously improving our platform based on students' needs and feedback.
